Mosic Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026 · Version 1.9

Welcome to Mosic Player (the “App”). We respect your privacy. Mosic Player is an offline local music player designed for users who organize and play their own audio files on iPhone and iPad. The App does not provide online music streaming, social features, or account registration.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not actively collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or contacts), and does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

To provide core playback and library features, the following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • Music library data: audio files you import (copied into the App sandbox), collection metadata, song titles/artists when available from file metadata, lyrics files, favorites, and sort preferences;
  • Custom cover images: photos you optionally choose from your photo library as collection covers (processed and stored locally);
  • Playback state: last played track, playback position, playback mode, sleep timer settings, and listening statistics;
  • App preferences: theme/skin selection and similar UI settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Import, organize, and play your local audio and lyric files;
  • Display collections, favorites, player UI, mini player, and personal statistics;
  • Remember playback progress and preferences between sessions;
  • Support optional custom collection covers selected from Photos.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Storage and Security

Library and preference data are stored primarily in the App sandbox and system preferences on your device. They are not uploaded to servers we operate.

Uninstalling the App typically removes local App data. If you rely on imported music, keep your own backups of original files.

4. Permissions

  • Files / Document Picker: used when you choose audio files or folders to import. Access is limited to items you select.
  • Photo Library: requested only when you choose a custom collection cover. Images are processed locally and are not uploaded by us.
  • Background Audio: used so playback can continue while the App is in the background or the screen is locked.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location permissions for its core features.

5. Third-Party Services

Opening the privacy policy, App Store product page, or write-review page may use Safari / App Store links provided by Apple. Those interactions are governed by Apple’s policies. Aside from system capabilities, the App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, push, or social SDKs.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience who manage their own local music libraries. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, please use the App with a guardian’s guidance.

7. Your Rights and Controls

  • You can delete imported songs, collections, custom covers, and clear local playback statistics inside the App;
  • You can revoke Photo Library access in iOS Settings;
  • You can remove remaining App data by uninstalling the App;
  • Original music files outside the App remain under your control.

8. International Users

The App is intended for users outside mainland China and processes data on-device. Because data stays local, cross-border transfer of your library by us does not occur.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is available in the App under Profile → Privacy Policy, and at this page. For material changes, we will update the effective date above.
