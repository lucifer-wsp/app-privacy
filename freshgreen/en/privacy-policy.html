FreshGreen Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 29, 2026 · Version 1.0

Welcome to FreshGreen (the “App”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information related to you. FreshGreen is an offline plant-care game. Core gameplay runs on your device, and no account registration is required.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not actively collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, precise location, or contacts), and does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

To provide gameplay and in-app purchases, the following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • Game save data: plant growth stage, season and weather state, virtual time, inventory counts, check-in records, and more;
  • App preferences: animation toggle, sound toggle, onboarding completion status, etc.;
  • Purchase status: whether Hibernation Privilege is unlocked (written locally after Apple StoreKit verification);

2. How We Use Information

  • Calculate plant progress and state changes while you are offline;
  • Display the home scene, inventory, check-in, and settings;
  • Verify Hibernation Privilege purchases and restore purchases via Apple In-App Purchase.

3. Storage and Security

The data above is stored primarily on your iPhone or iPad (app sandbox and system preferences). It is not uploaded to servers we operate.

When you uninstall the App, local saves are usually removed by the system. We recommend backing up your device if needed.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple-provided capabilities. Their data handling is governed by Apple’s privacy policies:

  • App Store In-App Purchase (StoreKit): purchases and restores of Hibernation Privilege are processed by Apple; we do not receive your payment card details.
  • System calendar day: daily check-in is based on the device’s local calendar day and is not uploaded to our servers.

Aside from the above, the App does not integrate third-party analytics, push, or social sharing SDKs.

5. Permissions

The App does not request sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location.

Restoring purchases and loading product information may require a network connection and a valid Apple ID session, handled by the system and App Store.

6. Children

The App is intended for general audiences. If you are a minor, please use it under a guardian’s supervision and let your guardian decide on in-app purchases.

7. Your Rights

  • You can clear local save data by uninstalling the App;
  • You can manage purchases via iOS Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases (Hibernation Privilege is a one-time non-consumable purchase);
  • For Apple account data unrelated to us, please follow Apple’s official guidance.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions are available in the App under Settings → Privacy Policy. For material changes, we will notify you in the App when appropriate.
