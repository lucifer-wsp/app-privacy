About FreshGreen · Care Guide

Version 1.0 · Fully offline

FreshGreen is a lightweight plant-care game. Tend your plant through seasons and weather— sow, water, remove pests, and shelter from rain and snow. This guide explains how to care for your plant and what to do when things go wrong.

1. Getting Started

  • First launch: after the intro, tap Plant Seed on the home screen. You begin in spring, game day 1.
  • After permanent death: tap Replant to start over. “Elapsed …” resets to day 1; “Together” in the top corner is hidden while the plant is gone.
  • The status card shows season, weather, growth stage, elapsed game time, and the current time flow rate.
  • Progress is saved on your device. No network is needed to play (purchases and restore need a brief connection).

2. Seasons & Growth

Your plant moves through spring, summer, autumn, and winter as game days pass—about 365 days per year. Seasons and healthy stages correspond as follows:

  • Spring: seed, spring sprout
  • Summer: summer peak
  • Autumn: autumn shed
  • Winter: winter dormant (bare branches; no new pests)

Healthy stages cycle: Seed → Spring Sprout → Summer Peak → Autumn Shed → Winter Dormant, then spring again. Seed germination takes about 7 game days; after that, stages follow the astronomical calendar—spring 91, summer 94, autumn 92, winter 88 game days (the first ~7 days of spring are seed, then sprout).

During wilt or other debuffs, the card season still follows elapsed game days and the astronomical season.

3. Time & Weather (easy to mix up)

What are game days for?

  • How long you have been caring for the plant (“Day / Month / Year X”);
  • Growth stages, dehydration checks, outdoor rain/snow damage buildup, and random outdoor pests in spring/summer/autumn (not in winter).

Check-in uses your phone’s calendar day, not game days—once per day.

When does weather change?

Weather (sunny, cloudy, rain, snow) shifts about every 6 virtual hours. It does not wait for a “new day,” so you may see sun and rain on the same game day. Higher time flow makes weather feel faster in real time.

The card shows the real outside weather, whether your plant is indoors or out—you can still see “snowy” while indoors.

Time flow

  • Higher flow = faster game days. Time advances while the app is open; reopening applies offline time in one go.
  • Changing flow in Settings does not reset “elapsed …”; only replanting starts the counter at day 1 again.
  • After a very long time, the display switches to game months or years for readability.

4. Weather & Indoor / Outdoor

Outdoors: full rain/snow visuals and ambient sound. Long exposure in rain or snow can hurt the plant—move indoors when you can.

Indoors: no rain/snow damage; particles may still show, but no rain sound; no “move indoors” button.

  • Sunny / cloudy: no rain/snow damage; normal care.
  • Rainy: auto hydration outdoors; neglect can lead to pests or damage over time.
  • Snowy: slower growth; long outdoor exposure can build damage.
  • Dehydrated indoors while it rains/snows outside: tap Move Outdoors to hydrate; that recovery usually does not count as damage for the rest of that game day.

Tap Move Indoors or Move Outdoors anytime to switch.

5. When Something Goes Wrong

  • Dehydrated: about 4 game days without water—water, or move outdoors in rain/snow.
  • Pests: possible outdoors in spring/summer/autumn; bugs appear on the plant—use pesticide or the pest mini-game.
  • Wilt: dehydration, pests, or rain/snow damage untreated for about 7 game days—water, clear pests, or use Repair Fluid. While at risk, the card shows a wilt countdown.
  • Decline: wilt not treated; ~7 game day final countdown—use Repair Fluid or buy Hibernation Privilege (below).
  • Deceased: final countdown ends without Hibernation Privilege—you must replant.
  • Hibernation: with Hibernation Privilege, the final countdown leads to hibernation instead; wake with Repair Fluid.
  • Long absence: not opening the app for about 21 real days can also trigger wilt—visit often!

6. Daily Care

Water

Fixes dehydration and helps wilt. Watering outdoors in rain gives half benefit.

Remove pests

  • Pesticide: from inventory or home button—instant clear.
  • Pest mini-game: use four digits and + − × ÷ to hit the target—free clear on success.

Recover plant

When wilted, in decline, or hibernating, tap Recover Plant on the home screen. Repair Fluid restores the healthy stage for the current season.

7. Items

  • Nutrient Fluid: ~7 game days of easy mode—no dehydration, pests, or wilt; stacks for longer protection.
  • Repair Fluid: clears wilt, hibernation, and other debuffs; cannot revive a fully deceased plant.
  • Pesticide: clears all pests instantly.

Open Inventory from the home screen. Check-in rewards may also grant these items.

8. Daily Check-in

Tap Check-in once per calendar day for a random Nutrient Fluid, Pesticide, or Repair Fluid. Missing a day does not remove items you already have.

9. Sound

  • Ambient rain and other sounds need Settings → Sound on, and the plant must be outdoors.
  • With the iPhone Ring/Silent switch on silent, ambient audio does not play—turn silent off and raise media volume.

10. Hibernation Privilege (optional purchase)

One-time purchase, permanent: after the final countdown, the plant hibernates instead of dying permanently. You still need Repair Fluid to wake it.

Buy or restore in Settings. Cannot recover a plant already deceased; protects future runs only.

No pay-to-win required: regular watering, pest control, and check-ins are enough for long-term care.

May your plant thrive through every season.
