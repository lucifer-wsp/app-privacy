About FreshGreen · Care Guide

FreshGreen · Version 1.0 · Fully offline

FreshGreen is a lightweight plant-care game. Tend your little plant through changing seasons and weather: sow seeds, water, remove pests, and shelter from rain and snow as it grows from sprout to flourish. This guide covers core rules and how to use the app.

1. Getting Started

  • On first launch or after your plant dies, tap Plant Seed or Replant on the home screen.
  • The game uses virtual time for seasons and weather; the top bar shows the current game day and days together.
  • All progress is saved locally. No network is required to play (purchases and restore need a brief connection).

2. Growth Stages

Healthy stages follow the seasons:

  1. Seed → Spring Sprout → Summer Peak → Autumn Shed → Winter Dormant, then a new spring cycle.

If neglected, negative states may occur:

  • Dehydrated: about 2 virtual days without watering (faster outdoors in sunny weather).
  • Wilt Level 1: dehydration or pests untreated for about 7 days.
  • Wilt Level 2: Level 1 wilt untreated; a ~7-day countdown to permanent loss begins.
  • Deceased: countdown ends without Hibernation Privilege; progress is lost permanently.
  • Hibernation: with Hibernation Privilege, the plant enters hibernation instead; use Repair Fluid to wake it.

3. Weather & Indoor / Outdoor

Indoors: blocks rain/snow hazard exposure buildup; no “move indoors” prompt.

Outdoors: shows virtual weather effects; move indoors during rain or snow.

  • Sunny: photosynthesis bonus outdoors; faster dehydration outdoors; no hazard damage.
  • Cloudy: neutral; no extra effects.
  • Rainy: auto hydration; long outdoor exposure counts toward the 7-day hazard window; pests more likely.
  • Snowy: growth pauses; outdoor exposure counts toward the hazard window.

Tap Move Indoors or Move Outdoors anytime; the scene and status update immediately.

4. Core Care Actions

Water

Restores moisture and helps with dehydration and Wilt Level 1. Watering outdoors in rain gives half benefit.

Remove Pests (two ways)

  • Pesticide: use from inventory or home shortcut to clear pests instantly.
  • Free pest mini-game: a target (1–99) and four digits (1–9) are given. Pick operators (+ − × ÷) and digits in order, using each digit once, to reach the target; pests are cleared on success.

Recover Plant

When wilted or hibernating, tap Recover Plant on the home screen. With Repair Fluid, restore to the healthy stage of the current season.

5. Items

  • Nutrient Fluid: lasts 7 virtual days; keeps the plant healthy with no dehydration, pests, or wilt; stacks if multiple are used.
  • Repair Fluid: clears wilt, hibernation, and other debuffs; restores full health for the current season; cannot revive a fully deceased plant.
  • Pesticide: instantly clears all pests and related debuffs.

Open Inventory from the home screen to view counts and confirm use.

6. Daily Check-in

Tap Check-in on the home screen. Based on the device’s calendar day (not virtual game time), once per day. Random reward: Nutrient Fluid, Pesticide, or Repair Fluid, added to inventory. Missing a day does not reset your history.

7. Hibernation Privilege (optional purchase)

One-time non-consumable purchase. Prevents permanent plant loss. After Wilt Level 2 countdown, the plant hibernates instead of dying; Repair Fluid is still required to wake it.

Buy or restore under Settings. Cannot recover a plant already deceased; privilege protects future progress only.

No pay-to-win required! Regular watering, pest control, and check-ins are enough for long-term care.

May your plant thrive through every season.
